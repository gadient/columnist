# Columnist — Product Roadmap

> Last updated: 2026-09-11

**Columnist turns your meeting notes into an organized board — and answers questions about the
work once it's there.** This page covers what it does today and the directions it could grow in.
Columnist is an open-source project, not a service under active development: the directions below
are ideas for anyone who picks it up, not promises.

---

## ✅ What's built

- **Notes → cards.** Paste or upload a meeting note and Columnist proposes kanban cards — with owners,
  due dates, and priorities — that you review and approve before anything is created. Nothing is added
  without your say-so.
- **AI assistant (alpha).** Ask about your boards in plain language — what's overdue, what's blocked,
  what's due this week, what's in a given column, a quick summary. It **follows the conversation**, so
  you can ask a natural follow-up ("and which of those are mine?"). It only reads; it never changes
  your cards, and it tells you when it can't answer.
- **Five ways to see your work.** Kanban, Timeline, Eisenhower Matrix, Feed, and Focus — the same
  cards, viewed the way that fits the moment.
- **Light & dark themes.** Pick light, dark, or follow your system — your choice is remembered, and
  every screen themes together.
- **Workspaces, boards & rich cards.** Group boards into workspaces; cards carry priority, due dates,
  assignees, labels, story points, Jira-style keys, and dependencies.
- **Invite-only accounts.** Sign in securely and share workspaces with the people you invite.

---

## ⏭️ Natural next steps

- **Take the assistant out of alpha.** It answers board questions reliably; what remains is polish —
  faster, streamed answers and broader coverage.
- **Questions across time.** Beyond today's snapshot — "what changed this week?", trends and velocity,
  and how work is flowing.
- **Better team management.** Manage who's in each workspace, with clearer limits and controls.

---

## 🔭 Bigger directions

- **Roles & permissions.** Owner / member / viewer distinctions so a workspace can be shared more
  widely, safely.
- **Sign in with a company account (SSO).** One-click access for teams.
- **An admin & insights console.** A management view of activity and progress across boards and teams.
- **Deeper integrations.** Two-way Jira sync and connections to the tools teams already use.
- **Programmatic tool access (MCP).** A read-only MCP server already ships, for a single trusted user
  on your own machine. The next step is a per-workspace version that is safe to expose, so external
  AI assistants can query boards.
- **Smarter meeting intelligence.** Notice what changed between meetings, and help coordinate work
  across teams.
